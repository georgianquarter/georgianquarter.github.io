# About & Contact

Liverpool Georgian Quarter is a community website intended to celebrate the neighbourhood's heritage, share information and encourage constructive engagement with local issues.

The website is being developed as a resource for residents and others with an interest in the Quarter.

## Contact

For enquiries, suggestions or contributions, please email:

[contact@georgianquarter.org.uk](mailto:contact@georgianquarter.org.uk)

We welcome ideas for improving the website and information that may be useful to the wider community.
