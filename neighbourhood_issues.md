# Neighbourhood Issues

Information about matters affecting the quality of life, safety and environment of Liverpool's Georgian Quarter.

This section will provide residents with information about local concerns, engagement with public authorities and progress towards practical improvements.

## Areas of interest

- Community safety and antisocial behaviour
- Street cleanliness and environmental concerns
- Planning and development
- Highways, parking and public spaces
- Communication with local authorities

Information will be presented factually, with supporting documents where appropriate.
